Hi everyone! My name is Michael Haney, and welcome to my portfolio page. I am a Graduate Student Services Coordinator within the College of Sciences at UCF. With a background in creative writing and editing, I’ve recently developed a strong interest in research and digital humanities — particularly in exploring how technology intersects with literature and storytelling.

This portfolio showcases my work from DIG3171: Digital Humanities, including critical analysis projects, data visualizations, and digital scholarship experiments.

Mapping the Magic: A Digital Analysis of the Evolution of Fantasy Literature

An interactive research project exploring the evolution of fantasy literature through text-mining and data visualization using Voyant Tools. It analyzes how themes, language, and narrative forms shift from early mythic tales to modern fantasy works.
